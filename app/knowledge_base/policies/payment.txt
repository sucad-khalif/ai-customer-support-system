PAYMENT POLICY

1. Customers must provide valid payment information when placing an order.

2. Payment confirmation is required before an order can be processed.

3. Customers should contact human support if they experience a payment problem.

4. Payment issues may require verification by customer support.

5. Customers should provide their order ID when requesting assistance with a payment-related issue.

6. The company does not provide payment information through customer support unless it is necessary to resolve the customer's issue.
