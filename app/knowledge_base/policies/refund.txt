REFUND POLICY

1. Customers may request a refund for eligible orders.

2. Refund eligibility depends on the condition and status of the order.

3. Customers should provide a valid order ID when requesting a refund.

4. Refund requests may require review by customer support.

5. The company will provide further information about refund eligibility after reviewing the order.

6. Customers who need additional assistance may contact human support.
