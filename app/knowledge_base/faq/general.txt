GENERAL FAQ

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: Customers can contact human support when they need additional assistance.

Q: What information should I provide when contacting support?
A: Customers should provide their order ID and clearly explain their issue.

Q: Can I get help with my order?
A: Yes. Customers can provide their order ID and describe their issue to receive assistance.

Q: What should I do if I cannot resolve my issue?
A: Customers may contact human support for additional assistance.

Q: Can customer support help with different types of issues?
A: Yes. Human support can assist customers with order, cancellation, refund, delivery, and payment-related issues.

Q: What should I do if I do not have my order ID?
A: Customers should check their order information or contact human support for assistance.
